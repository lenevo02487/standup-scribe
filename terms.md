## 1. The app

Standup Scribe is a Jira Cloud app built on the Atlassian Forge platform. It turns standup notes or meeting transcripts that you paste into it into a per-person digest (yesterday / today / blockers, with the Jira tickets mentioned). Standup Scribe was created as an entry for the **Codegeist 2026** hackathon.
